Questions

Does Blinkbox hold my money or keys?

No. Your wallet lives in PayBox, protected by your passkey. Blinkbox prepares transactions and PayBox signs them for you. Blinkbox can't move anything on its own, and you can disconnect it any time from the bot or from PayBox.

What do I need to start?

Telegram, a free PayBox account (sign in with Google, Apple or email), and a little SOL for network fees. About 0.01 SOL covers many transfers; each naira cash-out uses about 0.002 SOL.

What does it cost?

Blinkbox adds no fee. You pay Solana network fees (a fraction of a cent) and, for naira, the live Paycrest rate with their fee included. You see the exact amounts before you confirm.

Can I use it in Nigeria?

Yes. Buy USDC with naira by bank transfer, and cash out to any of 174 Nigerian banks and wallets, including OPay, Moniepoint and PalmPay. (Card top-ups through MoonPay aren't available to Nigerian residents.)

Can I pay someone else's bank account?

Yes. Tap Pay a bank account, enter the naira amount and their account number. The bank shows you the real account name first, then you approve with your passkey and they receive exactly that amount from your USDC. Bank payments can't be reversed, so only pay people you know.

How do @username payments work?

Link your Telegram username or X account in /handles. Telegram usernames are confirmed by Telegram itself, and X accounts by posting a one-time code. Anyone can then pay you by typing your @username, and you get a message when they do.

I have an AI agent. Can it use Blinkbox?

That's where Blinkbox started: it's also a PayBox plugin (currently in review for the PayBox marketplace) that lets agents like Claude and ChatGPT find and run Solana blinks with the same checks.
